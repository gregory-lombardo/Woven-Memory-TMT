\begin{abstract}
We propose that spacetime is not fundamental but emergent from a single underlying structure, a woven lattice of entangled event-bits (Chronons). We define the Memory Tensor $M_{\mu\nu}$ as the second derivative of mutual information $I$ in this lattice. We show that Einstein's curvature tensor $G_{\mu\nu}$ is directly proportional to $M_{\mu\nu}$ via $G_{\mu\nu} = \eta M_{\mu\nu}$, where $\eta$ is the forgetting constant. Quantum mechanics emerges as local braid dynamics, General Relativity as the large-scale elasticity of the braid. Black holes are reinterpreted as memory saturation cysts. We predict a discrete, measurable jitter in entangled atomic clocks and propose a mechanism for curvature inversion via induced forgetting (Letheon field). This work is presented as a dubious but falsifiable thought experiment.
\end{abstract}
