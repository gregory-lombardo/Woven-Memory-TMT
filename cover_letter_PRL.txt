Dear Editor-in-Chief, Physical Review Letters,

We are pleased to submit our manuscript entitled "Woven Memory: A Single-Tensor Unification of General Relativity and Quantum Mechanics via the Chronon Lattice" for consideration as a Letter in PRL.

In this work, we propose a deliberately speculative but rigorously falsifiable framework that addresses the core incompatibility between GR and QM not by adding dimensions or particles, but by removing the assumption of spacetime as a fundamental container. We introduce a single emergent quantity, the Memory Tensor M_mu_nu, defined from mutual information, and show that Einstein curvature G_mu_nu = eta M_mu_nu.

What distinguishes this submission:
1. Falsifiability: Predicts a discrete jitter at ~5e-44s in co-located optical clocks, testable by NIST/RIKEN within 5 years.
2. Parsimony: No extra dimensions, no new particles, only one constant (eta, the forgetting rate).
3. Resolution of paradoxes: Black hole information paradox resolved as memory saturation, not destruction.

We are fully aware of the unconventional nature of this proposal. We present it not as a claimed final theory, but as a "usefully wrong" thought experiment in the spirit of Wheeler's "it from bit" – a new direction that, even if falsified, constrains how entanglement can generate geometry.

We believe it will stimulate significant discussion in the quantum gravity community and is appropriate for the broad, high-impact readership of PRL.

The manuscript has not been submitted elsewhere. All authors approve submission.

Thank you for your consideration.

Sincerely,
Gregory Lombardo
Independent Research, London
On behalf of all authors
